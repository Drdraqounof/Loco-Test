How to Play:

  • Move: WASD or Arrow Keys
  • Shoot: Click anywhere on the screen
  • Objective: Destroy red enemies to earn points
  • Avoid: Colliding with enemies damages your health
  • Waves: Progress through waves as difficulty increases
  • Scoring: 10 + (5 × Wave) points per enemy
